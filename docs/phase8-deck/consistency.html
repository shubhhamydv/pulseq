05 / CONSISTENCY MODEL
Reliability is honest: effectively-once, not exactly-once
The system makes duplicates recoverable; business handlers must make side effects idempotent.
EFFECTIVELY-ONCE
Idempotency keysRepeated submissions resolve to the same logical job.
Retry policy + DLQFailures are bounded, jittered, observable, and manually replayable.
Execution historyEvery attempt is durable and attributable to a worker.
THE BOUNDARY
Exactly-once side effects across external systems are not guaranteed by the scheduler alone.
Handler responsibilityUse durable idempotency keys or transactional outbox patterns at the business boundary.
What the platform providesAt-least-once attempts with a practical safety envelope for well-designed handlers.
CONSISTENCY IS A CONTRACT BETWEEN THE ENGINE AND THE HANDLER
